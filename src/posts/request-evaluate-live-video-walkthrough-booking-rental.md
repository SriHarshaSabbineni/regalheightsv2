## The Era of Transparent Travel Booking

We have all heard the travel horror stories. You spend hours meticulously browsing third-party travel platforms, finally selecting an apartment with glossy, wide-angle photos and glowing descriptions. Yet, upon arrival, the reality is starkly different: the space is half the size, the "ocean view" requires leaning out of a window, and the natural light is non-existent. 

In the luxury accommodation sector, static photos—and even highly edited, pre-recorded promotional videos—are no longer enough to secure peace of mind. For discerning executives and luxury travelers, **the live video walkthrough** has become the gold standard of booking transparency. 

Here is exactly how to request, conduct, and evaluate a live video viewing to guarantee you get exactly what you see.

## Why Live Video Beats Pre-Recorded Tours

Many property listings now feature 3D matterport tours or slick, pre-recorded video walkthroughs. While these are helpful, they are still curated marketing materials. Lighting is optimized, angles are carefully selected, and less desirable elements are conveniently left out of the frame.

A **live video walkthrough** via platforms like WhatsApp, FaceTime, or Zoom is entirely unscripted. It provides a real-time, 1-on-1 interaction with your host, allowing you to direct the camera, ask immediate questions, and experience the property precisely as it stands at that very moment.

## Step 1: The Direct Booking Advantage

The first hurdle to requesting a live video tour is the booking platform itself. Major Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) intentionally block direct communication between the guest and the host prior to booking to protect their commission fees. 

To request a live walkthrough, you must leverage the **direct booking advantage**. By finding the property's official website (such as Regal Heights) and contacting the provider directly, you open a clear, unrestricted line of communication. 

**How to ask:** Keep it professional and straightforward. 
> *"Hello, I am looking to book the Royal Victoria Dock apartment for a two-week corporate stay. Before I finalize my reservation, would it be possible to schedule a quick 5-minute WhatsApp video call for a live walkthrough of the property?"*

Premium, trustworthy providers will always welcome this request. Reluctance or refusal to accommodate a live viewing is a massive red flag.

## Step 2: What to Evaluate During the Live Call

Once you are on the video call, do not just act as a passive viewer. You are there to inspect the space. Here is your evaluation checklist:

### 1. Spatial Awareness and Layout
Ask the host to start the call from the front door and walk slowly through the apartment. This allows you to gauge the true scale of the rooms, the ceiling height, and the actual flow of the floor plan without the distortion of a wide-angle camera lens.

### 2. Lighting and Views
Ask the host to open the curtains or blinds. 
* Is the apartment actually bright, or were the online photos heavily brightened in editing software? 
* Ask them to point the camera out the window or step onto the balcony. If the listing promises a "stunning waterfront view," verify it instantly.

### 3. Check the Essential Amenities
Direct the host to the areas that matter most for an extended stay. Ask them to open the fridge, point the camera at the washer-dryer unit, and show you the dedicated workspace. If you are traveling for a corporate assignment, confirming the physical presence and condition of these amenities is vital.

### 4. The Hidden Wi-Fi Test
A brilliant, hidden benefit of a live video call via WhatsApp or FaceTime is that it acts as a live stress-test for the apartment’s internet connection. If the video feed is constantly buffering, dropping in resolution, or disconnecting as the host walks into the bedroom, you immediately know the property's Wi-Fi might not support your heavy corporate Zoom calls.
