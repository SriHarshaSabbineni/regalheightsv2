## Unwinding After a Day at ExCeL London

Attending a conference, exhibition, or corporate event at the ExCeL London often means long days on the convention floor. However, when the panels conclude and the networking wraps up, you don't need to retreat to a cramped hotel room or face a long commute to Central London to find entertainment. 

If you are staying in a luxury serviced apartment in East London, you are perfectly positioned to explore one of the city's most vibrant and dynamic waterfront neighborhoods. Here is your ultimate after-hours guide to the Royal Victoria Dock.

## 1. Take to the Water: Wakeboarding and Paddleboarding

You might not associate corporate travel in London with extreme water sports, but the sheltered waters of the Royal Victoria Dock offer exactly that. 

Just a short walk from the ExCeL Centre, **Wakeup Docklands** is the city’s premier wakeboarding and paddleboarding destination. Whether you are an experienced rider looking to blow off steam or a beginner wanting to try paddleboarding as the sun sets over the city skyline, getting out on the water is an exhilarating way to decompress after a day of meetings. 

## 2. Soar Above the Thames: The IFS Cloud Cable Car

For a more relaxed but equally breathtaking experience, take a ride on the IFS Cloud Cable Car (formerly known as the Emirates Air Line). Located right at the edge of the Royal Victoria Dock, this cable car transports you 90 meters above the River Thames.

* **Sunset Views:** Timing your ride for golden hour provides spectacular, panoramic views of Canary Wharf, the Thames Barrier, and the historic Greenwich peninsula. 
* **Seamless Transit:** It is not just a tourist attraction; it is a highly efficient way to cross the river directly to the Greenwich Peninsula and the O2 Arena in under 10 minutes.

## 3. Dine in Style: Superyachts and Rooftop Bars

East London’s docklands boast a unique culinary scene, heavily influenced by its maritime surroundings. Bypassing standard chain restaurants, you can elevate your evening dining experience:

* **The Sunborn Superyacht:** Permanently moored at the dock, this luxury yacht functions as a high-end hotel and restaurant. Step aboard for a premium cocktail or a fine dining experience with a view of the water.
* **The Good Hotel Rooftop:** For panoramic views and a relaxed atmosphere, this floating hotel offers a fantastic rooftop bar. It is the perfect spot to host a casual post-conference drink with colleagues or simply enjoy a quiet evening overlooking the docks.
* **Local Waterside Eateries:** The promenade is lined with diverse, independent restaurants offering everything from authentic Middle Eastern cuisine to classic British gastropub fare.

## 4. World-Class Entertainment at The O2 Arena

If you decide to take the cable car across the river, you will land at the doorstep of the **O2 Arena**. Beyond hosting the world's biggest music and comedy acts, the O2 complex is an entertainment hub in its own right. 

Even if you don't have tickets to a main stage event, the venue offers:
* **Icon Outlet Shopping:** High-end retail therapy with premium brands at outlet prices.
* **Up at The O2:** An adrenaline-pumping guided climb over the roof of the iconic dome.
* **Boutique Bowling and Cinemas:** Perfect for a relaxed evening with your travel group or colleagues.
